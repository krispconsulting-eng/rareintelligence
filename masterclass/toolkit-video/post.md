# Accompanying post

Kris's own text, with contractions restored (the draft was dictated, which spells
"I am" and "I have" out in full). Post it verbatim. Mandar and MasterClass Executive
are tagged by Kris after publishing.

---

Mandar said something in one of the expert sessions about how we're all picking up
nuggets from these conversations and building a toolkit as we go.

That stuck with me.

So I made a video about it.

Twelve labs. One bag. Each one drops in as I get to it.

The toolkit's nowhere near finished. I'm only about a third of the way through.

I've missed heaps of sessions and I'm doing it at my own pace. One of the great things
about the programme is that they've been really generous and given us 12 months to work
our way through it all.

What's surprised me is which sessions stay with me.

It's rarely the ones I'd have picked as being most relevant to my work. I've sat in
sessions with no obvious connection to rare disease, health policy or consumer
engagement and still come away with something useful.

Sometimes I know exactly where it fits.

Sometimes it gets filed under: not yet.

And even a third of the way through, I'm already using it.

I've built a website. Built my own fundraising platform. I'm prototyping ideas, playing
with AI tools and automating pieces of work that used to chew through whole days.

Twelve months ago, I wouldn't have attempted half of this.

That's probably been the biggest shift for me. I'm much more willing to try things,
build things and work out what's possible.

Thanks to Mandar, and to all the experts who give their time and share what they know
in these sessions.

Still building the toolkit.
