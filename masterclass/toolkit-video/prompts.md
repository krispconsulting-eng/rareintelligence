# Background prompts

Paste the master direction, then the scene direction, into the generator. Generate at
9:16; five- or six-second clips trimmed to length, ten seconds for scene 9. Reuse a
seed or reference frame between clips where the tool allows it so the studio stays
consistent. Replace `[BUTTON COLOUR]` with the dominant colour of that scene's button,
read from the artwork.

Backgrounds carry motion and colour only. The bag, the buttons, the arrival flares and
all text are added in the edit, where they can be timed to the beat.
