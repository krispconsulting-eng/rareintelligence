# Voiceover

Music-led is the recommendation: the buttons and captions carry the story and the post
carries the explanations. If narration is wanted, use the 20-second script over the
open and the close and leave the button run to the music. Generate it in ElevenLabs or
record it; do a timing pass before locking the cut.

## Twenty-second narration

Over scenes 1 and 2:

I came into MasterClass Executive with deep experience in rare disease, lived
experience, health policy, research and HTA. Lab by lab, I am adding new capability to
that foundation.

Over scene 10:

Not replacing what I know. Strengthening how I use it.

## Longer scripts, for a cut of 50 seconds or more

Estimates at about 140 words a minute, with pauses between lines.

### Shorter narration, about 100 words, 45 to 50 seconds

I came into MasterClass Executive with deep experience in rare disease, lived
experience, health policy, research, HTA and meaningful engagement.

Each lab is adding another capability to that foundation.

AI strategy. Rapid prototyping. Human-led evaluation. Financial analysis. Responsible
AI. Product development. AEO. Voice and visual communication.

I am learning how to move from an unmet need, to evidence, to a prototype, to something
practical and valuable.

This is not about replacing what I already know. It is about strengthening how I use
it.

One lab at a time, I am building my toolkit and expanding what I can offer in my work.

### Full narration, about 155 words, 65 to 75 seconds

I came into MasterClass Executive with deep experience across rare disease, lived
experience, patient and consumer engagement, research, health policy and HTA.

I joined because I wanted to build on that expertise.

Each lab is adding another practical capability to my toolkit.

I am learning how to identify where AI creates genuine value, turn real problems into
testable tools, and use human expertise to define what good looks like.

I am building skills in financial analysis, product development, evaluation, AI
governance, answer-engine optimisation, voice and visual communication.

More importantly, I am learning how these capabilities work together.

How to move from an unmet need, to evidence, to a prototype, to something useful,
responsible and commercially viable.

I am not leaving my existing expertise behind.

I am adding new skills and perspectives that strengthen how I support organisations,
communities and the health sector.

One lab at a time, I am building it into the way I work.
