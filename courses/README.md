# Courses

The rare disease course product for Rare Intelligence: a catalogue of self-paced
online courses for families, advocates and the organisations that serve them,
plus the commercial and operational blueprint for selling them.

This work was designed separately and moved here so the courses live in the same
repository as the site and brand. It is content and strategy only. It carries no
visual design of its own: **course pages should be built in this repository's
existing brand**, using `assets/css/brand.css`, `assets/css/styles.css`,
`WEBSITE-PLAYBOOK.md` and `HERO-GUIDE.md`. Any brand or design language from where
this was authored has been left behind on purpose.

## The commercial model in one line

Free intro course, a flat $145 for every other course, program pathways, and a
whole pack (both programs) that is the only place three bonuses live: the Working
with Industry course, a toolkit of templates, and a full-pack Practitioner
credential. Full detail in `commercial/00-commercial-strategy.md`.

## Jurisdiction

Australian throughout: ACNC for charity governance, TGA for regulation, PBAC and
MSAC for reimbursement and HTA, the National Strategic Action Plan for Rare
Diseases for policy, the NHMRC Statement on Consumer and Community Involvement for
research involvement, and the Medicines Australia Code of Conduct for any
industry-facing delivery.

## One decision to make

The site currently positions Rare Intelligence around AI (the SIGNAL Method,
intelligence briefs). These courses are deliberately self-paced and not
AI-driven. Decide how the two sit together: courses as a distinct "Academy" or
"Courses" section of the Rare Intelligence site, framed to complement the AI and
advisory work rather than compete with it. That framing decision shapes the
navigation and the course landing pages, not the course content itself.
