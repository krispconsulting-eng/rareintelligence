Course 01 · Free intro · Families & advocates

Understanding the rare disease system

The map before the territory. In four short modules you'll see how diagnosis, care, research and access actually connect in Australia — and where a family or advocate can change what happens next.

Content current as at July 2026

What you'll be able to do
  • Describe the diagnosis-to-care-to-research-to-access pathway and name who decides at each step.
  • Locate yourself and your community in that system — and the points where advocacy has leverage.
  • Speak the language of clinicians, researchers and regulators well enough to be heard.
  • Choose your next course with a clear sense of where you want to build capability.
What's inside
01

The shape of the system

The whole pathway on one page: diagnosis, care, research, regulation and access — and how they feed each other.

02

Who holds which lever

Clinicians, researchers, the TGA, PBAC and MSAC, funders and patient organisations — what each one actually decides.

03

Where advocates change outcomes

The real points of influence, with examples of families and advocates who shifted a decision.

04

Your path from here

How the rest of the catalogue maps to the system, so you can choose what to learn next.

Who it's for

Families newly navigating a rare diagnosis, and advocates who want the whole map before they go deep on any one part. It is an orientation, not a specialist course — every other course in the catalogue assumes what this one teaches.

Why it's credible

Built from the major international patient-training programs — EURORDIS, EUPATI, Global Genes and others — and anchored to the Australian system: ACNC, the TGA, PBAC and MSAC, and the National Strategic Action Plan for Rare Diseases. It is kept current, with a visible "current as at" date on every module.
