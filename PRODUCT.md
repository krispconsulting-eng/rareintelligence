# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Rare-disease patients, advocates, and founders — people navigating a system with an average 5-year diagnostic delay across 7,000+ rare diseases affecting 300M people worldwide. They come in overwhelmed by scattered research, clinical data, and lived experience, needing it turned into something usable. Secondary audience: prospective Signal Circle members and advisory/speaking clients evaluating Kris Pierce's AI-for-rare-disease expertise.

## Product Purpose

Rare Intelligence is the marketing site for an AI advisory practice serving rare-disease leaders, built on AI, clinical evidence, and lived experience ("Rare by nature. Intelligent by design."). It exists to convert visitors into advisory clients, Signal Circle members, or Impact Project participants. Success is a visitor understanding the SIGNAL Method (Source → Interpret → Ground → Navigate → Act → Learn) and choosing a pathway: Work With Me, Membership, or Impact Project.

## Brand Personality

A trusted colleague, not a guru: direct, specific, grounded, warm, anti-hype. Never "transformative / revolutionary / game-changing." Restraint is the brand — one vivid-teal accent against a disciplined cool-blue palette (Deep Signal), no warm hues.

## Anti-references

Not hype-driven AI marketing (no "revolutionary," "game-changing" language). No warm hues anywhere in the palette. The zebra texture (universal rare-disease symbol) stays felt, not seen — never a loud, full-page pattern. Gradients are punctuation (bands, headers, footers), never full-page wallpaper.

## Design Principles

- Deep Signal system (`assets/css/brand.css` is the single source of truth) governs color, type, and texture — extend it, don't reinvent it.
- Name the tool, the workflow, the time saved — concrete over abstract, per `CONTENT.md`'s voice reminder.
- Restraint as identity: one accent color, texture felt not seen, gradients as punctuation only.
- Every page routes toward one of three pathways (Work With Me / Membership / Impact Project) — avoid dead-end pages.
- Content marked with real facts vs. placeholders (see `CONTENT.md` 🔴 items) must not ship as final until replaced with real data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No WCAG level specified yet. Given the audience includes people navigating health crises (potentially under stress, on mobile, with varying digital literacy), default to WCAG 2.1 AA and prioritize clarity and low cognitive load over visual flourish. `prefers-reduced-motion` must be respected (site already uses scroll-reveal via `main.js`).
