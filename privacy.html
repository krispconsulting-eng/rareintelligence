The fine print

Privacy Policy

Plain language, no surprises. Here's what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Rare Intelligence is an Australian advisory business that puts AI, clinical evidence and lived experience to work for the rare disease community. We're currently in our setup stage — this website, a newsletter and a membership (the Signal Circle) are the first things we're building. You can reach us any time at hello@rareintelligence.com.

What we collect

We keep it minimal. The forms on this site — the newsletter signup and the membership interest form — ask for your name and email address, and nothing else. If you email us directly, we'll also hold whatever you choose to include in that message.

We don't ask for, and don't want you to send us, health information through these forms. If you share details of your condition with us by email, we treat that with care, use it only to help you, and delete it on request.

How we use it

We use your name and email to do exactly what you asked us to do:

  • respond to your message or enquiry;
  • send you the newsletter or membership updates you signed up for.

We never sell your data. We don't rent it, trade it or share it with anyone for marketing.

Who processes the forms

When you submit a form on this site, it's delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service. Web3Forms passes your submission to us and doesn't use it for its own purposes. You can read their privacy policy at web3forms.com.

Unsubscribing

Every email we send is one you asked for — and one reply saying "unsubscribe" (or anything to that effect) is all it takes to stop them. No forms, no hoops.

Access, correction and deletion

Want to see what we hold about you, fix something, or have it deleted entirely? Email hello@rareintelligence.com and we'll sort it out — usually within a few days, and always without charge.

The legal bit

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. If you think we've got something wrong, please tell us first — we'll do our best to fix it quickly. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

As the business grows we may add tools (like analytics or a membership platform) that change how data is handled. When that happens, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
